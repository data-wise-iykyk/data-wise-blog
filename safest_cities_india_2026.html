Ranked: India’s Safest and Least Safe Cities in 2026

Visual Capitalist ranked America’s safest cities using WalletHub’s 41-metric index. Applying that same city-safety lens to India reveals a sharp divide between Tier-2 coastal/western cities and the Delhi-NCR belt.

Published November 23, 2026 · Data Sources: Numbeo Safety Index 2026 & National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB)

Key Takeaways

  • Mangaluru leads India with a 2026 Safety Index score of 74.8 (ranking inside the global top 50), driven by strong civic stability and low violent crime.
  • Gujarat dominates the top 5: Vadodara (#2, 69.4), Ahmedabad (#3, 68.3), and Surat (#4, 66.6) all outperform India's major tech and financial capitals.
  • Delhi-NCR occupies the bottom four spots: Delhi (40.9), Ghaziabad (41.8), Noida (43.9), and Gurugram (45.9) record the lowest safety scores nationwide.
  • Perception vs. Police Records: While Kolkata posts the lowest reported cognizable crime rate in official NCRB metro data (93.7 per lakh), citizen perception indices place southern and western Tier-2 cities higher on day-to-day street safety.

When Visual Capitalist mapped America’s Safest and Least Safe Cities in 2026, mid-sized suburban hubs like Overland Park, Kansas (70.19) and Boise, Idaho (68.59) easily beat sprawling metros like Houston and Detroit. Adapting that question to India produces a remarkably similar geographic pattern.

Because India does not publish a single WalletHub-style composite score combining crime, personal finance, and disaster risk, we cross-referenced the 2026 Numbeo City Safety Index (0–100 scale, where higher is safer) with official National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) metropolitan crime rates across 20 major Indian urban centers.

Tier-2 Cities Outperform Mega-Metros

Just like Overland Park and Boise beat New York and LA in the U.S., smaller Indian hubs (Mangaluru, Vadodara, Coimbatore) benefit from lower population density and tighter community policing.

The NCR Clustering Effect

Four contiguous cities in the National Capital Region—Delhi, Ghaziabad, Noida, and Gurugram—sit at the very bottom, weighed down by night-time walking safety concerns and property theft.

The NCRB Reporting Paradox

In official NCRB records, cities with high digital FIR registration (like Bengaluru at 677.2 per lakh) appear to have more crime than cities where minor offences go under-reported.

Why Mangaluru and Gujarat’s Cities Top the List

Mangaluru (74.8) stands nearly 19 points above the Indian urban average, outranking major Western cities like London, Paris, and San Diego on perceived street safety. High literacy rates, active neighborhood policing, and lower transient migration keep both violent and petty property crime low.

Similarly, Gujarat’s industrial corridor—Vadodara (69.4), Ahmedabad (68.3), and Surat (66.6)—captures three of the top four spots. Late-night commercial activity and dense CCTV coverage across urban intersections give residents higher confidence walking alone after dark compared to northern metros.

Official NCRB Crime Rates vs. Citizen Safety Scores

When evaluating safety in India, looking at a single metric can be misleading. Official NCRB figures measure registered police cases (FIRs) per 1,00,000 residents, where Kolkata consistently posts the lowest rate among India’s 19 mega-cities (93.7), followed by Pune (367.5) and Coimbatore (369.2). By contrast, crowdsourced safety indices capture lived experience—such as street lighting, harassment, and theft worries.
