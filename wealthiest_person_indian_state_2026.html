Ranked: The Wealthiest Person in Each Indian State (2026)

From Mukesh Ambani’s $105B Mumbai empire to regional industrial dynasties in Haryana, West Bengal, and Uttar Pradesh, see who tops the wealth chart across India.

Adapted from Visual Capitalist’s U.S. State Wealth Map · Data: Forbes India & Hurun India Rich List (2026 Estimates)

Key Takeaways

  • Extreme Western Concentration: Maharashtra’s Mukesh Ambani ($105.0B) and Gujarat’s Gautam Adani ($84.5B) hold more combined wealth ($189.5B) than the top billionaires of the next 16 Indian states combined.
  • India’s Richest Woman Tops Haryana: Savitri Jindal ($37.2B) leads Haryana (Hisar/NCR) through O.P. Jindal Group’s steel and power holdings, ranking fourth overall nationwide.
  • Distinct Sectoral Geography: Tech wealth anchors Delhi-NCR (Shiv Nadar) and Karnataka (Azim Premji), pharmaceuticals lead Telangana (Murali Divi), and consumer goods power Uttar Pradesh (Muralidhar Gyanchandani) and Kerala (M.A. Yusuff Ali).

1. The Mumbai–Ahmedabad Duopoly

Just as Elon Musk ($1T) and Jeff Bezos ($381B) tower over U.S. state rankings, Mukesh Ambani and Gautam Adani sit in a league of their own—each worth more than 2x India’s #3 billionaire.

2. Tech & Pharma Hubs

Unlike the U.S. where tech founders lead 8+ states, India’s tech billionaires cluster in Bengaluru (Azim Premji, N.R. Narayana Murthy, Nikhil Kamath) and Delhi-NCR (Shiv Nadar), while Hyderabad dominates pharma.

3. Heartland Consumer Empires

In populous non-metro states like Uttar Pradesh, Kanpur-based Muralidhar Gyanchandani built a $3.8B fortune selling Ghadi detergent to mass-market India—proving Tier-2 FMCG scale rivals tech.

How India’s Wealth Map Differs From the U.S.

In Visual Capitalist’s 2026 U.S. ranking, billionaires lead 46 of 50 states, spread across unexpected hubs like Arkansas (Walmart), Nebraska (Berkshire Hathaway), and Louisiana (Raising Cane’s). In India, billionaire residency is far more concentrated: over 65% of India’s 300+ billionaires live in just three urban clusters—Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, and Bengaluru.

The “Roots vs. Residence” Effect

Several of India’s wealthiest industrialists hail from states like Rajasthan (Lakshmi Mittal, Kumar Mangalam Birla, Radhakishan Damani) or Bihar (Anil Agarwal), yet operate out of Mumbai, Delhi, or London. When ranked strictly by primary domestic residence or corporate headquarters, industrial heartlands like Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and Madhya Pradesh are led by regional steel, mining, and manufacturing families.

Heavy Industry Still Rules Regional India

Outside the IT corridors of Bengaluru and Noida, old-economy sectors generate the biggest state-level fortunes. Steel, cement, iron ore, and textiles crown the richest families in Haryana (Savitri Jindal), West Bengal (Benu Gopal Bangur), Punjab (Rajinder Gupta), Jharkhand (Rungta family), and Chhattisgarh (Goel family).
