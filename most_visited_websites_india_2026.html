Ranked: The 20 Most Visited Websites in India (2026)

While Google and YouTube command 7.32 billion monthly visits in India, local giants like Flipkart, Hotstar, and Cricbuzz—and AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude—reshape the rankings compared to the global Top 40.

Published November 23, 2026 · Data Source: Semrush Traffic Analytics (Sept–Oct 2026 India Web Sessions)

Key Takeaways: India vs. The World

  • Alphabet's Duopoly Holds Firm: google.com (4.53B) and youtube.com (2.79B) generate 7.32 billion monthly visits in India—nearly 2.3x the combined traffic of the next 18 websites on the list.
  • ChatGPT Outranks WhatsApp Web & Facebook: Matching its #4 global rank, chatgpt.com draws 538.3 million visits in India, while developer-favorite claude.ai jumps six spots higher than its global rank to #15 (134.3M).
  • Instagram Doubles Facebook: Globally, Facebook (#3) beats Instagram (#5) nearly 2-to-1. In India, the script flips completely: Instagram (#3, 621M) attracts nearly double Facebook's traffic (#6, 313.3M).
  • Five Local & Cricket Powerhouses Break In: amazon.in (#7), flipkart.com (#8), hotstar.com (#10), indiatimes.com (#13), and cricbuzz.com (#17) replace global staples like TikTok (banned in India) and Temu.

1. How India's Web Habits Diverge From the Global Top 40

In Visual Capitalist's global ranking of the 40 most visited websites in 2026, U.S. and global portals like amazon.com (#13), tiktok.com (#11), and temu.com (#31) command billions of sessions. In India, regulatory bans (TikTok and Temu) and localized domains completely reshape the top tier.

Instead of amazon.com, Indian shoppers flock to Amazon India (amazon.in) at #7 with 266.7 million monthly visits, closely followed by homegrown rival Flipkart (flipkart.com) at #8 with 200.8 million visits. Even though the vast majority of Indian e-commerce happens inside mobile apps, browser traffic alone puts both retailers inside India's top 10 websites.

2. India Is a Disproportionate Growth Engine for AI & Developers

Globally, ChatGPT ranks #4 with 8.23 billion monthly visits—and India contributes roughly 6.5% (538.3 million visits) of that total, making it the fourth most visited website in the country ahead of WhatsApp Web and Facebook.

Even more striking is India's over-indexing on developer and productivity tools. Claude (claude.ai) ranks #21 globally, but climbs six spots to #15 in India (134.3M visits, or 8.5% of its global traffic). Similarly, GitHub (#18, 113.5M) and LinkedIn (#11, 166.9M) each account for roughly 8% to 9% of their entire global web traffic from India alone.

3. Cricket and Local OTT Beat Global Streaming

While netflix.com (#25 globally) draws a respectable 102.9 million monthly web visits in India (#19), it trails Hotstar (hotstar.com) at #10 (181.5M visits), which thrives on live cricket and regional entertainment. Meanwhile, Cricbuzz (cricbuzz.com) surges into the #17 spot with 114.6 million visits—proof that live ball-by-ball cricket scores remain one of the most resilient web habits in India.
